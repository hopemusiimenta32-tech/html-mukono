Welcome to Mukono

This website highlights some familiar places and useful services around Mukono. The website is designed to work well on phones, tablets and laptops.

Places to Know

Mukono Town

A busy town centre with shops, banks, restaurants and transport connections.

Uganda Christian University

A university in Mukono with students, learning facilities and community activities.

Seeta

A growing area near Mukono with homes, schools, shops and everyday services.

Everyday Services

Mobile Money

Airtel Money and MTN Mobile Money make sending and receiving money convenient.

Health Services

Pharmacies and health centres provide basic health support to the community.

Food and Restaurants

Mukono has restaurants and small food businesses serving students and residents.

Transport

Taxis and boda-bodas are common ways to move around Mukono.
